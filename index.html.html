ROBLOX GAME CONCEPT · v1

THE MAGNET

Loot spawns on a magnetic floor. One player is the Magnet — a walking gravity well that drags everyone toward the bottom of the bowl. You can't outrun it. You can only decide what you're willing to throw away.

8–12 players / server
3 min rounds
1 Magnet per round
5 loot tiers
6 retention engines
Roblox · low-poly
01

The loop, in one picture

Three loops stacked inside each other — a 3-minute loop, a 30-minute loop, a multi-day loop.

THE ROUND

Dive, loot, deposit, 3 minutes. Die and you lose only what you're holding. Instant restart — no punishment.

THE SESSION

Coins buy tools and jackets. Better gear = deeper runs = rarer loot. A visible power curve every 20 minutes.

THE BASE

Relics on shelves print coins while you're offline. Come back richer than you left. That's the daily habit.

02

The map: a crater, not a tower

Height = safety. Depth = money. The Magnet lives at the lowest point.

Rim

3 vaults. The only safe ground. Where you bank and where the World Relic must be dragged.

Terraces

Mid-tier loot. Pillars block the Lash (not the pull) — the cover you use.

Bowl

Best loot in the game, and the lowest point. The Magnet spawns here and owns it.

Why not a vertical tower? The pull is distance-based — three floors up makes "how close am I?" meaningless, and gravity plus pull creates physics chaos. A crater keeps height as a readable danger gradient while staying one continuous arena.

The magnetic floor rule

Loot doesn't just sit there. Anything left on the ground slowly drifts downhill toward the Magnet, and the Magnet eats it when it arrives.

Three consequences, all good: the floor is never cluttered, players can't farm one safe pile forever, and there's a visible timer on greed — that Prime over there is sliding away right now.

Carrying it

Hand slots6 (upgradable to 10)
Weight penalty−3% speed per item
Visibilitystacks on your back, seen from afar
On deatheverything in hands is gone

There is no coin on the floor and no money in your hands. Loot is a physical object you can lose. Coins only exist after you deposit.

The core tension: the danger ring is 35 studs when you're empty-handed and 55 studs when you're loaded. A spot that was safe on the way in becomes a death trap on the way out. That single widening circle is the entire game.
04

The Magnet

Whoever earned the most last round becomes the monster. Winning is the reward.

Base kit

Walk speed8 (players: 16)
Catch radius6 studs
Passive auraradial pull, ignores walls
Incomecatches + flat 300 / round
Can bank loot?no — separate scoring path

Abilities

Pulse · 8s3× pull for 0.4s + stagger
Lash · 6shook one target, reel 2s (LOS only)
Field Flip · 12srepel for 2s — shove people
Eat1.5s animation, then 3s digest

Digest halves the pull for 3 seconds. One player can never eat the whole lobby — the monster has downtime.

Role rotation is the retention trick. Nobody is stuck as the prey. Everyone gets to be the monster, because the top earner of the round is the one cursed with it next round.

The rule that keeps it a game: money buys you mistakes, never safety. Every jacket still dies if it stands still inside the ring — the best suit just buys you 4.5 seconds instead of 1.2.

  • Pull resistance beyond the 35% cap

The museum

  • Your base is a separate map from the arena.
  • Each pedestal holds one relic — 3D, glowing, slowly spinning.
  • Any player can walk in and look — that's the flex.
  • Offline income ticks on this shelf, capped at 8 hours.
  • Set bonus: 5 of a kind → ×1.25. Full page → ×2.0.
08

The World Relic — the mid-round event

Every 3rd round, a car-sized relic spawns in the bowl. You cannot pick it up.

1
It spawns loudly at 90 seconds — announcement, map marker, audio cue. Everybody knows.
2
Grab a rope (E). 1 carrier = 8% speed · 2 = 30% · 4 = 60%. It's a team object.
3
Everyone on a rope is 25% slower and can be eaten. Getting caught snaps your rope off.
4
The Magnet can claim it too — touch it for 3 seconds and it's absorbed forever, with a permanent curse tier.
5
Bank it at a rim vault (1.5s). Everyone on a rope gets 25,000 coins + a Haul Seal (Epic, +8/sec).
6
The MVP — most seconds on the rope — keeps the World Relic itself: Mythic, +40/sec, plus their name on the Hall of Fame board.
The MVP rule is deliberate. Four people pulling the same rock, each secretly wanting the most time on it. Free drama on top of the chase. And if the round ends first, it despawns and nobody wins — that's the "so close" moment that makes them queue again.

Why it hooks players

Six engines running at once. This is the answer to "why do they come back tomorrow?"

THE ONE DIFFERENCE

In Steal an Egg, nothing can ever be taken from you. It is calm, safe, and idle. Retention is enormous; excitement is near zero.

In The Magnet, another player can empty your hands. Same gacha, same idle layer, same collection — plus a heart rate.

That's the entire bet: take the reward structure of an idle game and put a monster in the middle of it.

10

The economy

Active play must always beat idling — otherwise it stops being a game.

WHERE COINS COME FROM
  • Depositing loot (active)
  • Shelf reliquaries (idle)
  • Set bonuses (collection)
  • World Relic payouts (event)
WHERE THEY LEAVE
  • Tools — consumed even on death
  • Jackets and permanent upgrades
  • Vault pedestals
  • Cosmetics
WHY IT DOESN'T INFLATE
  • Death costs the round's earnings
  • Consumables burn on use
  • Idle is capped at 8 hours
  • Relic income is capped by shelf slots
The single most important number: the danger ring. 35 studs empty-handed, 55 studs while loaded. Every other value in this document is tuning. That one is the game.

Stop at step 4 and playtest with 4 friends. If nobody ever drops loot, the weight penalty is too low. If nobody ever complains about the pull, it's too weak. That one dial decides whether the design lives.
